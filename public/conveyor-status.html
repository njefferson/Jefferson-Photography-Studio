Work in progress — where it has got to

The run of work after 2.62. It starts by putting right what 2.62 left behind: a red check on production, wording in the records that should not be there, stale directions to a menu that moved, and a Share icon that draws as an empty box on the iPad. Then it answers what came back from the device the same day: red edges on metal under Aerochrome, a LUT list you have to leave to see anything, a top bar that wraps, and masks that cannot carry the controls you want inside them.

This page is updated in place. The link does not change.

  • On staging v2.62.16: the export edge fix and two new rows on the device test page. Your pass came back good; it waits for your go.

The stages

  • Done Production's checks are green again. One word in the notes failed the check that looks for a person referred to by pronoun. It only runs on the server, so nothing local caught it, and 2.62 went out while it was red, because the deploy does not wait for the checks. Fixed; every check now passes on production's tree.
  • Done The records say what was wrong and what it measured, and nothing else. Some decision records and notes quoted the conversation they came from or named who asked. More than 330 places were rewritten as the engineering fact, and four details that were simply wrong were corrected in the same pass.
  • Answered Why Aerochrome draws red around steel against the sky. Not the metal's reflectance, for the colour outside it: one black post is blue on one edge and red on the other. Red and blue sit about half a pixel apart in the editing copy, the lens adds its own offset toward the corners, and Aerochrome's mixer turns that difference red at about twice its size. The round spots are Sky colour smoothing, and they go at 0. Then your own raw files answered it for your photograph. On NIR_3461, the frame you exported, the red on the wires and pylons is Aerochrome's foliage band: it picks by colour, and a thin dark wire against white cloud falls inside the foliage colours. With Foliage at 0, the strongly red pixels above the horizon go from 16,430 to none, the round spots go with them, and the grass loses its red too. Lining up the colour channels changes nothing on that frame. So the clean answer is Foliage applied only to the ground, which is the foliage-inside-a-mask you asked for and a stage of the mask work. Lowering Foliage cleans the metal today, at the cost of the grass.
  • Researched Masks that mask, with every control acting inside them. The model is Capture One's: pick a mask and the ordinary tabs follow it. No editor masks everything; geometry, the decode, the look choice and the LUT file stay whole-photo, with the reason shown in words. It can be built in stages, each its own release, and the first stage also fixes a tool aimed at a mask ignoring what that mask subtracts. The mask record is rewritten around this, with its stages and the three choices it leaves you; pictures of the panel come before any code.
  • Next Masks, stage 2b: the rest of the colour and tone tools on a picked mask. HSL, the grade, luminance with LUT strength, the curves, then shadow colour, each its own change on the held build. A mask's curve is its own and applies after the whole photo's, as in Lightroom and Capture One; the others add to the whole photo's where the mask reaches. Black and white stays for the whole photo, as it does in both: a mask's own Saturation at its lowest is the local route. Written out in the mask record first.
  • Next The top bar and the start card. Decided: the bar is Home, Undo, Redo, Hold: Before, a "?" for Help and More, which holds Reset, Full view, Open, Quick look and Develop unattended. The histogram over the photograph folds to its own icon where it sits. The start card's regrouping comes after the bar. Measured: the bar needs about 1020 pixels and gets 736 on a landscape iPad, so it wraps; in portrait it takes four rows and leaves the photo 14% of the screen; the start card hides the practice photos below the fold. Shorter labels or icons do not fix it, because the bar carries three kinds of action at once. Three layouts were drawn in the real app and sent as pictures; each gives a one-row bar at every size. A check that holds the bar to one row is in the repository and fails today, as it should. The side panel that folds away stays a separate question.
  • Built, held LUTs you tap through on your own photo, and a None for looks. A tap shows on the photograph straight away, tapping the chosen one again takes you to its strength, a run of taps is one step to undo, and the LUT comes back with the photograph after the iPad closes the tab and you resume. None is now the first of the looks: it takes the look off, back to the photograph's own balance, and the next photographs open without one; a LUT stays. Checked on a raw and a camera JPEG: the photograph comes back exactly as it opened. Every walk passed. It goes to staging with the mask work.

  • Built, held The wire fix, moved up: masks, stage two, with Foliage first. Built: a row of mask names above the tabs; pick one and Exposure, Warmth, Hue shift, Saturation, Contrast and the Foliage band act on it, with its name in the heading and Back to whole photo beside it. To take the red off wires or cladding: set the lens profile on the Corrections tab first (it takes the hot spot's pink and much of the cladding's red on its own), then pick a gradient over everything above the grass and lower its Foliage saturation. In the full export the red above the horizon went to none on both frames, with the lens profile on. The cost: the red trees on the horizon go grey with it, the gradient's soft edge takes a little red off the grass near the horizon, and a pylon leg standing in the grass stays red. It waits behind v2.62.16 with the rest of the held build. What follows is the record from before it was built. Foliage limited to a mask is the stage that fixes the red wires, pylons and cladding: on your building, Foliage at 0 takes the red on the cladding from 13,561 pixels to none and the red on the grass from 87,991 to none, and the grass needs it while the building does not. It was ranked behind work it does not need: eight other controls that were to become per-mask first. It needs only the first stage (built, held) and the switch that makes the tabs follow a picked mask, so the switch comes next with Foliage as its first per-mask setting, and the eight follow. Settled by convention: a mask's value is an offset on the whole photo's, the swap and the channel mixer stay whole-photo, and the masks sit as a row of chips above the tabs. On the way, a real fix: a colour mask joined to another mask changed the screen and not the saved photo. With that fixed, a gradient over everything above the grass, joined to a colour mask on the red with its saturation at 0, takes the red above the horizon in the full export from 233,425 pixels to 43,733 and leaves the grass alone; the red trees on the horizon go dark with it. It needs the fix, so it is usable once the held build ships, not today.

Found, and not fixed

Things measured on the way through that are real and are not yet repaired. They are written down so they are not re-discovered as new.

  • A mask joined to the one above still shows its own Brightness, Contrast and Saturation sliders, which nothing reads once it is joined. Fixed in the held build: those sliders left the mask panel, and the tabs act on the picked mask's group.
  • Foliage can only be set for the whole photo. It selects a colour, not a place, so bright cladding on a building reddens along with the grass. Fixed in the held build: pick a mask and lower its Foliage.
  • Masks do not survive the tab closing. The rest of the edit comes back after the iPad closes the tab, and the masks, with anything set on them, do not. Help says so. It matters more now that a mask carries the wire fix.
  • The round pink spots differ between the screen and the saved photo when masks are on. With the wire workaround applied they stay pink on screen and are faint in the export; with no masks the two agree. They come from Sky colour smoothing, which runs after the masks. One cause is fixed in the held build (the screen's smoothing did not follow a mask's changes); whether that is all of it has not been measured again. Owned by the Aerochrome smoothness work.
  • Full view shows on the start screen and does nothing there, and a pressed Histogram is drawn exactly like the Open button. Owned by the top-bar work.
  • A LUT does not survive the iPad closing the tab. The rest of the edit comes back and the LUT does not. Fixed in the held build, which has not reached you yet.
  • The deploys installed a range of wrangler versions. The hub's deploy failed once on a wrangler release published minutes earlier that the package registry was not yet serving; a re-run went green. The same step ran 4.86.0 here and 4.139.0 in the hub on the same night. Fixed on main: each deploy names the version its last green deploy ran, so neither changes what it deploys with, and the next deploy of each is read to confirm it.
