Work in progress — where it has got to

The run of work after 2.62. It starts by putting right what 2.62 left behind: a red check on production, wording in the records that should not be there, stale directions to a menu that moved, and a Share icon that draws as an empty box on the iPad. Then it answers what came back from the device the same day: red edges on metal under Aerochrome, a LUT list you have to leave to see anything, a top bar that wraps, and masks that cannot carry the controls you want inside them.

This page is updated in place. The link does not change.

Where the builds are

  • Production v2.62, carrying two documentation commits since, which change nothing you see.
  • On staging v2.62, the same app as production. Nothing new is waiting there yet.
  • Last verified green f7ed4b4: all seventeen checks in the Gates run passed, and the deploy for that exact commit passed. The run before it had failed at check ten, which meant the seven after it had never run on 2.62 at all. A page cannot carry the identifier of the commit that writes it, so this always names the one before.
  • Next version 2.63, when masks gain their own amounts. The fixes before that take the increment digit and no version bump.

The stages

  • Done Production's checks are green again. One word in the notes failed the check that looks for a person referred to by pronoun. It only runs on the server, so nothing local caught it, and 2.62 went out while it was red, because the deploy does not wait for the checks. Fixed; every check now passes on production's tree.
  • In progress The records say what was wrong and what it measured, and nothing else. Some decision records and notes quote the conversation they came from or name who asked. They are being rewritten as the engineering fact, and four details that were simply wrong are being corrected in the same pass.
  • Researching Why Aerochrome draws red around steel against the sky. The wires themselves render red, soft round red spots sit along them, one face of each pylon is red, and the sky seen through the lattice is grey. Being checked against published sources and reproduced on the practice photos stage by stage, with every render opened, before any answer about reflectance is given.
  • Researching Masks that mask, with every control acting inside them. How Lightroom, Capture One, darktable and others let a selected mask take the ordinary controls, which of this app's controls can do that and at what cost, and how sharp a mask's edge can be. This reopens an option the mask record had rejected, and the record is being rewritten around it.
  • Next The top bar and the start card. Measured: the bar needs about 1020 pixels and gets 736 on a landscape iPad, so it wraps; in portrait it takes four rows and leaves the photo 14% of the screen; the start card hides the practice photos below the fold. Shorter labels or icons do not fix it, because the bar carries three kinds of action at once. Three layouts will be drawn in both themes at three sizes and shown to you as pictures. The side panel that folds away is part of the same question.
  • Next LUTs you tap through on your own photo. Comparing eighteen LUTs today means eighteen round trips through a full-screen list. The usual shape is a browser beside the photo, each tile drawn from your picture, None first, tapping the chosen one again for its strength, and import, share and delete kept apart. Browsing must not fill the undo history, and a LUT must survive the iPad closing the tab. Four layouts will be drawn and shown as pictures.
  • Queued One staging build of small fixes. The Share icon on the start page, which draws a character the iPad has no font for; five messages that still send you to "Profiles & LUTs"; the licence text the four fonts require; and a line saying where the app's colour numbers come from.
  • Queued The checks that should have caught the red. The privacy checks run before a commit instead of only on the server, and the records refuse wording that says who asked.
  • Queued Masks, in code. First, a tool aimed at a mask respects how that mask was combined; then the ordinary controls act inside a selected mask, in stages, each one its own device pass.

Found, and not fixed

Things measured on the way through that are real and are not yet repaired. They are written down so they are not re-discovered as new.

  • Two old offline copies stay on the device after an update. The device report from staging lists the 2.58.12 and 2.58.15 caches alongside 2.62, which should have removed them. To be checked in the next round of walks.
  • A tool aimed at a mask may ignore an area subtracted from it. Found by reading the code, not yet seen in a render. It is the first code step of the mask work.
  • Foliage can only be set for the whole photo. It selects a colour, not a place, so bright cladding on a building reddens along with the grass. Owned by the mask work.
  • Full view shows on the start screen and does nothing there, and a pressed Histogram is drawn exactly like the Open button. Owned by the top-bar work.
  • A LUT does not survive the iPad closing the tab. The rest of the edit comes back and the LUT does not. Help says so; the LUT work changes it, using the way a saved look already keeps its LUT.
