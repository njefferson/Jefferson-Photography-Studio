Work in progress — where it has got to

The run of work after 2.62. It starts by putting right what 2.62 left behind: a red check on production, wording in the records that should not be there, stale directions to a menu that moved, and a Share icon that draws as an empty box on the iPad. Then it answers what came back from the device the same day: red edges on metal under Aerochrome, a LUT list you have to leave to see anything, a top bar that wraps, and masks that cannot carry the controls you want inside them.

This page is updated in place. The link does not change.

The stages

  • On production Edits to the open photo were lost if the page reloaded or the tab was closed before you moved to another photo. They were saved only on a switch, Home or a LUT. Now they are also saved when the page goes to the background and within a second of each change. In v2.63.33.
  • On production On a photo turned on its side, the look's sky was found on the wrong edge. Every look that uses the sky works it out on the unturned frame: sky colour smoothing, sky depth, sky saturation, the depth lift, shadow light, and the tiles and batch exports made with them. White balance, exposure, lens correction, the hue bands and your own Sky masks get the turn right. Three of the seven frames Aerochrome's sky depth was tuned on are stored turned. Fixed in v2.63.28 for the open photo, its tiles and a batch, with a flip, Undo after a turn, a new Gradient and Level on a mirrored photo. Clouds inside the look's sky on a turned photo now take the cyan tint upright photos already get (069).
  • On production Keep straight after Done sat silent for minutes, and a second press added the set twice. Reported from the PC in Firefox after ending a session of 222 photos. Now the card rises at once, the wait happens only when the browser says the set will not fit, and it says so with a count; a photo refused for room meanwhile is stored again; a slow file is offered to be skipped; a second open is refused in words. Found and fixed with it: a saved photo dropped together with photos deleted an open session without asking. And a batch no longer waits on the browser's question about keeping data. In v2.63.26.
  • On production The lens correction opens on again, remembered per lens. On 22 cell-tower frames a curve from your own flat frames matched every one. Off, the middle of the sky was a grey or red-grey patch; on, it evens out. The strength you choose is kept for the lens at every aperture. In v2.63.23.
  • On production The first launch after the release froze for about a minute, and nothing said why. Measured on the PC: building the editor's picture code from scratch took 42 seconds while the page waited, and 557 ms on the iPad. The 28 MB every release downloaded was a second cost, about two and a half minutes on the PC's connection. Both are fixed on staging in v2.63.21: an update keeps what the device already has and downloads only the rest, the notice counts the files, and the start screen answers and says it is preparing the editor while the code is built. The first reading, from a PC in Firefox: the update kept 211 files and downloaded 7, and the start froze for 44 seconds in silence. Firefox offers no way to build off the page and reused nothing from its last start, so it pays the build every time, and the words went up only for a build the app expected to be slow. Now a browser whose last build was slow gets the words before it starts, on staging in v2.63.23. Making the build itself faster is still to come.
  • Stopped The third answer to the sky sliders tinting clouds was measured, and stopped before a render. Build the sky map's squares from sky only. It fixed the squares along the sky's edge on six of seven photos with sky, and failed on the seventh, where every square that still disagreed traced to red treetops the look's selection itself counts as sky. What is left is the selection, not the map.
  • Stopped The second answer to the sky sliders tinting clouds was researched, and rejected before a render. Judge a pale pixel by what surrounds it, read off the app's own sky map. Measured on eight photos: the map's squares disagree with their neighbours along every edge of the sky, not inside it, so the rule would act in a band along every treeline, roofline and horizon, which is where the first answer grained the sky. And the inside of a cloud reads the same as the centre of an uncorrected hot spot, so no colour rule can spare one and not the other. Nothing was built.
  • Stopped The first answer to the sky sliders tinting clouds was built, and taken out. One rule for the three sky stages: what arrives without colour leaves without colour. It kept clouds white under Sky depth and took the red haze off treetops, and it brought the pale speckle back into a clear blue sky at the look's own settings, grained the sky above the horizon, and broke cloud edges into dots. Those were checks it could fail, so it failed. It never reached staging.
  • Stopped A learned sky selection was built, and taken out. Two models were fused into the look's sky selection and exported beside today's build. Whole frame against whole frame it was worse on every photo with sky: clouds turned blue-grey, the steel in the near pylon went dull with red glows, and sky was missed above a roof and beside a tree. It was better only on the photo with no sky. It never reached staging. What was found on the way is written in decision 052, so it is not built again as it was.
  • Done The research that should have come first is written down. What the film physically did, from Kodak's own data sheets; what practitioners, LUT makers and the published science say about getting Aerochrome from a camera like this one. No LUT or recipe produces it from a 665–720 nm conversion; the film's red is dense dye from foliage being dark in the visible, and its dark sky a deliberately slow infrared layer. It is in IR-SCIENCE, sections 4b-viii and 4b-ix, and it reshaped the Aerochrome plan into a model of the film (066).
  • Done Production's checks are green again. One word in the notes failed the check that looks for a person referred to by pronoun. It only runs on the server, so nothing local caught it, and 2.62 went out while it was red, because the deploy does not wait for the checks. Fixed; every check now passes on production's tree.
  • Done The records say what was wrong and what it measured, and nothing else. Some decision records and notes quoted the conversation they came from or named who asked. More than 330 places were rewritten as the engineering fact, and four details that were simply wrong were corrected in the same pass.
  • Answered Why Aerochrome draws red around steel against the sky. Not the metal's reflectance, for the colour outside it: one black post is blue on one edge and red on the other. Red and blue sit about half a pixel apart in the editing copy, the lens adds its own offset toward the corners, and Aerochrome's mixer turns that difference red at about twice its size. The round spots are Sky colour smoothing, and they go at 0. Then your own raw files answered it for your photograph. On NIR_3461, the frame you exported, the red on the wires and pylons is Aerochrome's foliage band: it picks by colour, and a thin dark wire against white cloud falls inside the foliage colours. With Foliage at 0, the strongly red pixels above the horizon go from 16,430 to none, the round spots go with them, and the grass loses its red too. Lining up the colour channels changes nothing on that frame. So the clean answer is Foliage applied only to the ground, which is the foliage-inside-a-mask you asked for and a stage of the mask work. Lowering Foliage cleans the metal today, at the cost of the grass.
  • Researched Masks that mask, with every control acting inside them. The model is Capture One's: pick a mask and the ordinary tabs follow it. No editor masks everything; geometry, the decode, the look choice and the LUT file stay whole-photo, with the reason shown in words. It can be built in stages, each its own release, and the first stage also fixes a tool aimed at a mask ignoring what that mask subtracts. The mask record is rewritten around this, with its stages and the three choices it leaves you; pictures of the panel come before any code.
  • After the Aerochrome work Masks, stage 2b: the rest of the colour and tone tools on a picked mask. The colour mixer, the grade and the Sky band are on staging: pick a mask and each moves only where the mask reaches, with every export checked against the screen. Luminance with LUT strength is next. HSL, the grade, luminance with LUT strength, the curves, then shadow colour, each its own change. A mask's curve is its own and applies after the whole photo's, as in Lightroom and Capture One; the others add to the whole photo's where the mask reaches. Black and white stays for the whole photo, as it does in both: a mask's own Saturation at its lowest is the local route. Written out in the mask record first.

Shorter labels or icons do not fix it, because the bar carries three kinds of action at once. Three layouts were drawn in the real app and sent as pictures; each gives a one-row bar at every size. A check that holds the bar to one row is in the repository and fails today, as it should. The side panel that folds away stays a separate question.

Found, and not fixed

Things measured on the way through that are real and are not yet repaired. They are written down so they are not re-discovered as new.

  • The confirmation after applying a LUT from the manager, and after saving without location, is never seen. It is put inside a dialog that closes on the next line.
  • A strip tile of a photo not yet opened shows Aerochrome's foliage pale pink where the opened photo is red.
  • One saved photo opened on its own while a session is open replaces that session without asking.
  • In Firefox on the PC, every start holds the page for 44 seconds while the editor's picture code is built, because that browser can neither build it off the page nor reuse the last build. The words saying so are on production. The change meant to make the build faster did nothing: your PC measured 44,527 ms in Firefox on 2026-09-28. Two causes remain, and the test page will measure which.
  • A photo far along a long strip cannot be opened until the thumbnails reach it. Reported from the PC with 222 photos. Noted for later; it gets a record and a rank first.
  • The layout of the editor's grid of tabs was reported from the device: eleven tabs three to a row with the last row one short, and the explanations switch alone beneath them. Noted for later; it gets a record and a rank first.
  • On a darker photo the look's sky selection takes in a tree, and Aerochrome paints it scarlet (073). Found on the IR Chrome frame, 2.4 stops under: the selection covered 46% of the frame against 21% on the same scene exposed normally, and Sky saturation boosts anything the selection holds that has colour. The normally exposed frame shows it on willow strands in front of the sky. Recorded and ranked after 052; research comes first.
  • A photo shot well under opens grainier than a normal one (072). Automatic noise reduction removes a fixed share of the noise, and a dark frame starts with more. Raise Noise reduction by hand for now. The one design tried was refuted before it was built.
  • Stickers on a photo you returned to after resuming a session were missing from its export until the Stickers tab was opened. Fixed on staging, in v2.63.21.
  • The update strip can offer an older waiting version as new, because it asks whether the waiting version is different, not whether it is newer; taking it deletes the newer version's half-finished download. Read from the code, not yet reproduced. Fixed on staging, in v2.63.12: it offers only a newer version.
  • The host answers any missing file with the start page and a success code, so a release landing in the middle of a download can leave a page stored under a program file's name until the next release. Checked against production. Fixed on staging, in v2.63.12: a missing address answers 404, read on staging.
  • Stickers placed on a photo whose edit is restored are missing from its export until the Stickers tab has been opened, and the export says nothing. Read from the code, not yet reproduced.
  • A faint vertical colour band sits at the same place on two different photographs under Aerochrome, x 5230–5270 of the full export, red minus blue falling about 10 levels across it. Absent under Pink IR. Cause not established.
  • Aerochrome's sky selection takes in a pale building face on NIR_3466, with a hole in it, so a sky depth darkens the building. Part of 052.
  • An infrared LUT applied over a swapping look undoes the swap. The infrared LUTs available (Rob Shea's, Kolari's, PictureFX's) all expect the frame before any swap, and the app applies a LUT last. Measured on Rob Shea's "IR RB Swap", which is an exact red-blue swap.
  • A mask joined to the one above still shows its own Brightness, Contrast and Saturation sliders, which nothing reads once it is joined. Fixed on staging, in v2.63: those sliders left the mask panel, and the tabs act on the picked mask's group.
  • Foliage can only be set for the whole photo. It selects a colour, not a place, so bright cladding on a building reddens along with the grass. Fixed on staging, in v2.63: pick a mask and lower its Foliage.
  • With a mask picked, the Sky band was dimmed and said nothing about why where you were looking. The reason sat at the top of the Colour tab. Fixed on staging, in v2.63.7: the Sky band follows a picked mask, and the line under "Editing" says dimmed controls stay with the whole photo, wherever the panel is scrolled.
  • Double-tapping a slider that shows a picked mask's value sent it to the whole photo's opening value, so a mask's Foliage saturation went to +1 rather than to no change. In v2.63 as first staged. Fixed on staging, in v2.63.7, and the gesture no longer loses a second tap that arrives while the photo is still redrawing.
  • Every new mask lightened the photograph with nothing moved. A new mask started with a value of its own (a brush or radial brighter by a third of a stop, a Sky or Colour mask more saturated), which since the switch sat on tabs where nothing said so. Fixed on staging, in v2.63.7: every value starts at no change, and the tint still shows a new mask's area while you place it.
  • Aerochrome's sky smoothing comes out very slightly different the first time it is drawn. The smoothing map built when the look is applied differs from any later rebuild with the same settings by at most 6 levels in 255, and fewer than one pixel in ten thousand moves more than 4. Invisible, but it breaks a byte-for-byte check, which is how it was found. Why is not yet known.
  • Sky inside a pylon's lattice stays outside the Sky mask. The Sky mask reaches sky only along sky-coloured paths, and the struts cut every one. The route today is "Add to it" with a Colour mask on the sky, which leaves specks and takes the lit steel edges. A fill, a stroke inside the struts that takes the similar sky around it (the way Capture One's Magic Brush works), is recorded as decision 065 and ranked eighteenth; under Aerochrome it reaches the look's own sky only once 052 lands.
  • Masks do not survive the tab closing. The rest of the edit comes back after the iPad closes the tab, and the masks, with anything set on them, do not. Help says so. It matters more now that a mask carries the wire fix.
  • The round pink spots differ between the screen and the saved photo when masks are on. With the wire workaround applied they stay pink on screen and are faint in the export; with no masks the two agree. They come from Sky colour smoothing, which runs after the masks. One cause is fixed on staging (the screen's smoothing did not follow a mask's changes); whether that is all of it has not been measured again. Owned by the Aerochrome smoothness work.
  • Full view shows on the start screen and does nothing there, and a pressed Histogram is drawn exactly like the Open button. Owned by the top-bar work.
  • A LUT does not survive the iPad closing the tab. The rest of the edit comes back and the LUT does not. Fixed on staging, in v2.63.
  • The deploys installed a range of wrangler versions. The hub's deploy failed once on a wrangler release published minutes earlier that the package registry was not yet serving; a re-run went green. The same step ran 4.86.0 here and 4.139.0 in the hub on the same night. Fixed on main: each deploy names the version its last green deploy ran, so neither changes what it deploys with, and the next deploy of each is read to confirm it.

Everything here happens on your device. This page is a record of work in progress and carries nothing about your photographs.
