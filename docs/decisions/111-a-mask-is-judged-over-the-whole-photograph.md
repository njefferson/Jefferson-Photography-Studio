# 111 · A mask is judged over the whole photograph

## Context

**The goal.** A mask is made from samples placed on the photograph and takes what the samples hold; it copies to other photographs with what the copy would take shown first; a wrong copy is put right by samples placed there. This end result was confirmed on 10 November 2026, before this record and before the measurement it rests on, and is written here in this record's own words. It is record 110's goal.

What this record settles. Two things. First, how a mask made from samples is judged: only over the whole photograph, against a truth the method did not set, in pictures that were opened, and never by a share of the pixels inside the samples. Second, which method makes the mask: none is chosen, because no method measured met the result on any of the twenty-two named subjects of eight photographs, the 2.79 recipe among them.

Where it stands. Record 110's first stage is on staging as 2.79: at a count the test page fits, for each placed name, counts on nine cues weighed as naive Bayes, draws the weight, the cut at a half and the cut refined to edges, and prints the share of each placed name's counted pixels a mask takes. Every figure the page prints of a mask is a share of sampled pixels; what a mask takes among the pixels no sample holds was in no figure. Record 006, the mask by subject, has waited on a reading of what a browser on a device can run.

What was measured, on 11 November 2026, in the container and not on the device:

- **Eight photographs of the app's list**, each drawn once as the editor opens it with no look, at the working copy's 2,800 by 1,864: NIR_3461 (lattice pylons over a pale field), NIR_3466 (a tall building, a field, a road), NIR_1827 (conifers under cloud), NIR_1651 (a branch against sky and cloud, stored turned), NIR_3742 (a forest, a beach, a lake and a marina), NIR_3705 and NIR_3710 (two towers dressed as conifers among pale shrubs), and NIR_1873 (a forest slope and a pool, no sky). NIR_3742, NIR_3705 and NIR_3710 carry the samples placed on the test page on 10 and 11 November; the other five carry samples placed for this measurement. Twenty-two named subjects in all.
- **Seven planted pictures** with known regions, each region filled by mirror tiling from one window of one of those frames, with sixteen regions in all and every truth written at 10:53 on 11 November, before any mask was made.
- **Three inputs**: the picture as it opens; the natural-hue re-mapping of the science file's section 4b-x; and a bright-only form in which bright pink foliage goes green and darker reddish built things keep their colour.
- **The bar.** On a real frame: the thing sampled taken to its edges, no region of another kind larger than a hundredth of the frame taken, and no band of the wrong kind along an edge wider than four pixels at the frame's own size. On a planted picture: overlap over union of 0.9 or more with the mask cut at a half, and a widest band of wrong pixels along the boundary of four pixels or less.
- **The entries.** The 2.79 recipe, through the app's own module, rendered Refined to edges on the picture as it opens. For each learned kind, two variants fixed by their mean planted overlap in coarse form before any frame was judged, each put through one edge stage fixed by a stated rule before the maps were made: the plain guided filter with the picture's grey as its guide, radius 16, regularisation 0.001.
- **The judging.** Three agents that built none of the maps each opened, for every frame and subject of their share, a comparison picture of eight panels (the frame with its samples, the recipe, two entries of each learned kind) and a crops picture of the same panels at full size at two places with a mark four pixels wide, before reading any builder's note, and marked every panel met or not met by the bar with what was seen by place.

What it found. **No entry met the result on any of the twenty-two frame-subjects.** On the planted pictures, after the edge stage, the recipe met the bar on 1 of 16 regions and every learned entry on none. The planted figure did not foretell the frames: the recipe's mean planted overlap in coarse form was the highest of all, 0.9752, and on the frames it took wrong regions as large as a fifth of the frame. Each fault is set out under Rejected.

## Options

1. **Every mask is judged over the whole photograph, and no method is chosen yet. Chosen.**
   - **The judging.** A method is a candidate for the test page or the editor only when, on photographs of the app's list carrying named samples, its masks meet the bar above on most named subjects, judged in pictures opened at the frame's size and at full-size crops by someone who built none of the maps, beside planted pictures whose truths were set before the method ran. A planted score is read beside the frames and never in place of them, and a share of sampled pixels is never given as a measure of a mask.
   - **No method.** Every entry met the result on 0 of 22 frame-subjects: the recipe; the classifier fitted on DINOv2-small features, on the natural and the bright input; MobileSAM prompted by every sample's centre at once, on the input as it opens and the natural one; and the scene-class model with each class given to the name whose samples hold most of it, on both inputs. On the planted pictures after the edge stage, the recipe met the bar on 1 of 16 regions (one region of plant-three, overlap 0.9934, widest band 2 pixels) and no learned entry on any. What each got wrong is under Rejected.
   - **What it rests on, picture by picture.** NIR_3466's Foliage was the nearest any panel came: MobileSAM held the field's edge against the road within a few pixels and failed on the lower parts of fence posts about as wide as the four-pixel mark, while the recipe, the fitted entries and the scene-class entries took the road. On NIR_3710's Sky MobileSAM took no wrong region over a hundredth of the frame and left bands of sky of 5 to 45 pixels over the shrub tips and round the towers. On NIR_3461's Built things the recipe took swaths of cloud, about a fifth of the frame; MobileSAM filled each pylon's silhouette with the sky of its lattice; the fitted entries drew blobs wider than the steel; the scene-class entries took almost nothing. On NIR_1827's Sky the recipe's edge followed the tree line closest and left a dark cloud of a little over a hundredth of the frame; MobileSAM drew a smooth curve leaving bands of 30 to 60 pixels between the tops; the fitted entries left the bright cloud over the tree line, about a sixth of the frame. On NIR_3742's Foliage MobileSAM left the trees down the right border, about six hundredths of the frame, and the recipe took the beach beside the pale tree. On NIR_1873's Water no entry followed both the open pool and the water seen between the near pine branches.
   - **What the edge stage's own measure says is the limit.** Measured alone on the planted pictures from a coarse map made from the truth itself on cells of 28 pixels, the stage fixed by the rule met the bar on 1 of 16 regions where the coarse map as it is met it on 6; the weighted guided filter met it on 6, the band decided again by the recipe's cues on 2. From a map misplaced by half a cell, the fixed stage met it on none, and only the cue stage moved a misplaced line back to the boundary, leaving patches where a region's own texture varies. No stage lowered the median widest band below the coarse map's own, 10 pixels from a right map and 22 from a misplaced one. On every planted boundary but one the two regions' grey hardly differs, and a filter guided by the grey has no edge there to follow. So a coarse map brought up to full size, even where it is right, does not reach a band of four pixels through a guided filter on the picture's grey on most planted boundaries; the test's cells of 28 pixels are the size of the scene-class model's and the feature model's cells here. What the measure leaves open is a method that decides near the frame's own pixels, or an edge stage that reads more of the picture than its grey, as the cue stage did when it alone moved a misplaced line.
   - **What it holds.** The test page's stage of 110 stays as built. 110's editor stage is not built from any method measured here. Record 006 stays where it is, with its reading now in it.

## Rejected

- **The classical stand-in: the 2.79 recipe as the mask.** Record 006 rejected a classical stand-in on 5 July 2026: subject and background have no classical signal to key on in these images, colour evidence is void under infrared, and an approximation would ship a control that works on some photographs for reasons nobody can state. This instrument showed it of the recipe, which keys on lightness, colour differences, texture and edge alignment fitted from the samples. It met the result on 0 of 22, though its planted overlap was the highest, 0.9752, and its edges where the kinds were right the closest of any panel. Its faults are of kind, not of edge: it takes whatever shares the samples' tone and leaves parts of a subject in another tone. On NIR_3461 it took swaths of cloud for Built things, about a fifth of the frame, and every pylon's steel for Sky; on NIR_3466 the road, about six hundredths of the frame, and a band of cloud, about four hundredths, for Built things; on NIR_3710 the pale rock, about a fifth of the frame, for Foliage, and the dark twiggy row across the frame for Built things; on NIR_3742 about fifteen hundredths of the frame that is not built, the beach and the water round the dock, for Built things; on NIR_1873 the boulder shore and the scree for Foliage. It left the sky between the towers of NIR_3705, about seven hundredths of the frame, and of NIR_3710, about a twentieth. On NIR_3705's textured towers it drew a speckle of taken and left.
- **MobileSAM prompted by points.** 0 of 22. A point selects what is joined to it, so it leaves parts of a subject that hold no sample: NIR_3742's trees down the right border, the low shrubs standing on NIR_3710's rock, NIR_1873's foreground pine clusters, NIR_1827's far left trees, the pale annex of NIR_3466, the leafless tree at NIR_3705's left border. It takes a subject as a filled outline, so a lattice is filled or left whole (NIR_3461's near pylon, about a tenth of the frame) and a tree line is rounded over by smooth curves of 30 to 100 pixels (NIR_1827, NIR_3742). Its planted overlaps sat near 0.99 on most regions with no band under 5 pixels. Its points do not travel, so a copy could show nothing on another photograph until it was marked there, which the goal's showing first cannot meet, as record 110 found of a mask solved on each photograph from its own marks. Its encoder is over the host's limit for one file.
- **A classifier fitted on DINOv2-small features.** 0 of 22. It decides on patches of 28 frame pixels, and after the edge stage its edges keep the grid's rounded shape, standing 30 to 100 pixels off the boundary on most frame-subjects and up to about two hundred along a horizon or a tree line, with halos of 40 to 120 pixels round NIR_3461's pylons. It leaves parts of a subject unlike the samples: the bright cloud over NIR_1827's tree line, about a sixth of the frame; cloud at NIR_3710's top edge; the pale shrub tops of NIR_3705. It took the road with the field on NIR_3466 and the water between the branches with the forest on NIR_1873, and left strips along frame borders whose cause was not measured. It is the only kind measured that carries a fit: from NIR_3705 to NIR_3710, one scene, the carried masks took near what the second photograph's own took but for Foliage, which left about an eighth of the frame to no subject; from NIR_3461 to NIR_3466 the carried Built things took nothing; from NIR_3742 to NIR_1873 the carried Water and Foliage took a quarter or less of what their own took. Making its features took 3.6 to 37.4 seconds a picture in the container, and the ONNX copy run names no licence of its own.
- **A scene-class model with classes given to names.** 0 of 22, with 0 of 16 planted regions and two at an overlap of 0. Its faults are of class: it takes every region of a class its samples' names were given and leaves a subject whose class went to another name. It took NIR_3466's road with the field, NIR_3742's beach with the water (about five hundredths of the frame), NIR_3705's towers with the foliage, and the shrub tops of NIR_3705 and NIR_3710 with the sky; it took nothing at a half for Built things on NIR_3461, NIR_3705 and NIR_3710 on the natural input. Its weights were read only as a PyTorch file and ran here through a rewrite of the forward pass that was not compared value by value with the publisher's own run.
- **SlimSAM.** Its planted means were 0.62 to 0.83, against MobileSAM's 0.87 to 0.97 on the same pictures, and it was not judged on a frame; it is the weaker of the two models of points measured, and nothing shown says it would not fail as MobileSAM did, by what its points join.
- **Choosing the nearest entry anyway.** MobileSAM came nearest on one frame-subject of twenty-two. A method that fails on most is a control that works on some photographs for reasons nobody can state, which is record 006's reason against a classical stand-in and holds as well of a learned one.
- **Judging by a share of sampled pixels.** A share of pixels inside the samples says nothing of what a mask takes over the rest of the photograph, and the field scores over the whole mask against a whole truth.
- **Judging by the planted pictures alone.** The recipe's planted figure was the highest and it met the result on no frame. The planted pictures hold no surface of one kind that shares another kind's tone, no layer of one kind in front of another, and no region of a subject that holds no sample, which are the cases that broke the entries on the frames.
- **Choosing another edge stage after seeing the frames.** The stage was fixed by a rule before the maps were made; the weighted filter met the bar on more planted regions from a right map, and no stage lowered the median band below the coarse map's own. A stage chosen after the frames were seen is chosen by eye.
