# The separation measure, computed over the gather of 8 November 2026

This file is written by a harness that reads the 41 frame files under docs/research/separation/gather/ and nothing else. It decodes, draws and fetches nothing. It ranks 82 settings of white balance and channel remap by how far apart the sky and the foliage of one rendering sit in hue. No setting is singled out in words here: the ranking is the numbers, and the build plan for record 084's panel reads them.

## The measure, in words
- Populations: the gather labels each thinned pixel as sky where the app's own sky selection reads at least 0.5, else neutral where its saturation at the opening render is under 0.13, else foliage where the vegetation axis (red less green, over green, at the opening gains) is at most minus 0.05, else other. The 0.5, the 0.13 and the minus 0.05 are the gather's own figures and no source gives them.
- Distance: the Hellinger distance between the sky population's 72-bin hue histogram and the foliage population's, over the pixels at or above the saturation floor 0.13, each histogram normalised to its own total. It is the square root of one minus the sum over the bins of the square root of the product of the two shares. It is 0 for the same histogram and 1 for histograms sharing no bin, it is a metric, and it assumes no shape for either population.
- Beside it: the circular mean hue of each population from the bin centres and its resultant length from 0 to 1, the smaller arc between the two means from 0 to 180 degrees, and each population's share of its pixels at or above the floor.
- Control: the neutral population's share above the floor and its circular mean and resultant length, which say whether a setting coloured what has no colour. The whole frame's circular mean is carried beside them as the illustration IR-SCIENCE.md section 6 names.
- Colourless: a population with fewer than one in a hundred of its pixels at or above the floor is colourless at that setting and the distance is undefined there. It is written as undefined and never as a number. A population with no pixel at all, as when the sky selection found no sky, is absent and the distance is undefined in the same way.
- One cast: a setting in a frame is called one cast when sky and foliage are both coloured and every coloured label among sky, neutral, foliage and other has its circular mean within one bin width of 5 degrees of every other. That 5 degrees is the instrument's own resolution and a figure of this file.
- The four figures printed with every result: 0.13, 0.5, minus 0.05 and one in a hundred.
- Means are reported for sky and foliage only where the population is coloured. For the neutral control and the whole frame a mean is reported whenever any pixel is at or above the floor, so the control can be read where it is small.

## The measuring space
- The distance above is computed in the display space the page measures in: the hue of the 8-bit display-P3 pixel the drawn path ends with, in 72 bins of 5 degrees, counted over pixels at or above saturation 0.13.
- Beside it is the distance in the chroma plane the gather also holds: CIE chroma and hue from the same 8-bit values read through the sRGB transfer and primaries, 72 bins of 5 degrees, counted over chroma at or above 5, a population being colourless there by the same one-in-a-hundred rule. Reading P3 values through sRGB is a measuring convention and not a claim.
- The gather's pixels come from the app's computed path, the sRGB to P3 conversion applied after 8-bit rounding, without the lens flat and without a lens curve, every third pixel of every third row of the half-size decode. The device's sweep measures in the space its drawn frame ends in, so a difference between the two is read on the press before any number is compared across them.

## What the gather's files hold
- Frames: 41. Settings in each: 82. Frame and setting pairs: 3362. Frames whose settings differ in order or name from the first frame's: 0. Histograms whose bin sum differs from the stated count of pixels above the floor: 0.
- The labelling rule recorded in the frame files, as the frames state it: sky weight at least 0.5, neutral saturation under 0.13, foliage index at most -0.05, chroma floor 5.
- The vegetation axis's sign, read against IR-SCIENCE.md 4b-ix: that section derives the axis as the visible share times (visible over infrared, less one), with a clear sky's red-to-infrared ratio at roughly 3 to 4 and foliage's at roughly 0.1, so foliage sits on the negative side. The gather's rule puts foliage where the index is at most a negative figure, and 41 of 41 frame files record a negative threshold. The sign agrees with the derivation. The harness reads the sign from the recorded rule and does not recompute the axis, since the files hold counts and not pixels.

- The setting ranked first has a median distance of 0.9841, a median arc of 66.2 degrees, and a neutral control with a median of 88.3% of its pixels at or above the floor: at that setting the population that has no colour at the opening render is coloured too.

- The opening balance leaves the sky colourless or absent in 19 of 41 frames with the swap on, so the distance there is a reading over 22 frames and not the whole set.
