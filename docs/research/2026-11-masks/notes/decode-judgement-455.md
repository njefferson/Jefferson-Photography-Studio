# Judgement of step 455: NIR_3742, NIR_3705, NIR_3710, NIR_1873, and the three copied recipes

Judged by an agent that built none of the masks, from the pictures alone, each opened before any note or report was read; no step's note or report was read at all. The bar is the plan's: a mask meets the result where no connected region wrongly taken, and none wrongly left, is larger than one hundredth of the frame, and where its edge leaves the thing's own edge by more than four pixels at none of the crops its picture shows. Each mask is marked on both clauses; the mask from the decode is then marked against the mask from the drawn picture with one word, better, same, worse or broken.

How the figures were read. Sizes of a region are read off the overview panels, 512 pixels wide in a comparison picture (one panel pixel about 5.5 frame pixels a side) and 384 wide in a copied-recipe picture (about 7.3), so each is a rough figure; one hundredth of the frame is about 52,000 pixels, a square about 230 a side. Distances at the places A, B and C are read off the crops, which are at the frame's own size, against the four-pixel mark. The masks are judged at the half cut the yellow and cyan lines draw; a tint under a half is described where it shows and not counted as taken. The percentages in the lines are the pictures' own titles.

## What this judgement cannot show

- How the masks look drawn on the device. These pictures were made in the container through the harness of step 453, by a software renderer, and not by the test page on the device's own graphics chip; a mask the page draws on the device is not shown here.
- Exact sizes. Every region size is read by eye off a panel 512 or 384 pixels wide and is rough; three marks rest on a region read near one hundredth (NIR_3742 Water under the boats, NIR_1873 Water among the boughs, the NIR_3461 to NIR_3466 Foliage copy at the building's tower), and each could go the other way. The worse on NIR_1873 Water is one of them.
- Whether two pieces are one region. Where pieces lie close, the panels cannot settle whether they connect.
- Soft values. The masks are judged at the half cut; a tint under a half over the forest in NIR_3742 Water and over the upper forest in NIR_1873 Water is not counted, and a mask applied at its soft values would show it.
- The edge away from the three places. Each comparison picture shows the edge at three places only; the edge clause is judged there and nowhere else. The copied-recipe pictures show no crops, so a copy's edge is not judged at all.
- What a subject includes, where the samples do not say. The dark stems and shade at the base of shrubs and forest are counted here as part of the foliage, and so as left out by a Foliage mask and wrongly taken by a Built things mask; both ways are judged alike on it, so no comparison word rests on it.
- More than four frames. This judgement covers four of the eight frames and three pairs; step 454's covers the rest.
- How either mask serves a look or an edit. Only what a mask takes and leaves is judged.
