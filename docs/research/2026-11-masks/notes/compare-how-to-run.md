## The order step 440 runs them

1. `R/compare/entries.mjs trial`. Reads the map folders' index files and fixes the entries by figures: for each learned kind found as R/maps-<kind> (a folder holding an index.json, at any depth), the first entry is the candidate (kind, variant, input) with the highest mean overlap over union at a half over its planted masks in coarse form, a candidate holding all 16 planted masks (one for each name each planted picture's samples carry) or, where none does, the candidates with the most masks; the second is the best candidate on another input than the first's. The scene-class kind's entries are its two inputs, from R/compare/classes/index.json. The recipe's entry is its rendering Refined to edges on the picture as it opens, carried smoothly, from R/maps-recipe/index. A kind with no index file or no planted mask has no entry and the pictures say so. Writes R/compare/entries.json (the pictures read it), R/compare/entries.md (each entry with the figure that fixed it, and every candidate's figure) and R/compare/trial/index-trial.json, which is the list of every entry's coarse masks, a list of { entry, picture, subject, input, file }.
2. For every row of R/compare/trial/index-trial.json whose entry is not `recipe`, run `R/refine.mjs` (the edge stage and setting R/edge/chosen.txt names) on the row's coarse mask file with the row's picture, and write the result as R/final/<entry>-<picture>-<subject slug>.png; for the `recipe` rows the file goes in as it stands, with no edge stage. Write R/final/index.json: a list of { entry, picture, subject, input, file } with the file the final mask's full path. The input a row names is the input the entry's coarse mask was made on; the picture the edge stage is guided by is the one the step 436 notes name for that input (the picture as it opens, unless they say otherwise).
3. `R/compare/planted-scores.mjs R/final/index.json R/compare/planted-scores.md`. Runs the scorer (R/score.mjs) as a child on every planted row, four at a time, and writes one line for each with the bar beside it (overlap over union 0.9 or more and a widest band of 4 pixels or less), then for each entry the count meeting the bar. Its plant runs the scorer on a mask made from a truth's own region and must read 1.0000 and 0 px.
4. `R/compare/count.mjs <mask.png> <truth.png> <subject>` on two final planted masks, and compare its overlap over union with the line planted-scores.md holds for the same mask. It is a separate count (taken right, taken wrongly, left wrongly, then overlap over union); on a recipe mask of plant-three Sky it read 0.9349 where the scorer read 0.9349, with taken right 1,825,283 and left wrongly 125,617 adding to the scorer's 1,950,900 subject pixels. It does not measure a band.
5. For each frame and each subject its samples name (the 44 pictures listed by `R/compare/index-md.mjs` with an empty folder are the names to write: comparison-<picture>-<subject slug>.png and crops-<picture>-<subject slug>.png, the slug being the subject in lower case with hyphens), under R/final/pictures:
   - `R/compare/compose-comparison.mjs <picture> <subject> R/final/index.json <out.png>`: eight panels in two columns, 1400 wide and 1966 tall. Panel 1 the frame as it opens with its samples drawn; panel 2 the recipe's entry; panels 3 and 4, 5 and 6, 7 and 8 the two entries of each learned kind in the order entries.md gives (features, then points, then scene-class, as the folders sort). Each mask panel is the mask drawn over the frame as the sheet maker draws it (magenta tint by weight, yellow outline at a half) under a title with kind, the model where it is not the kind, a short variant, the input and the share of the frame taken at a half. A kind with no entry draws NO ENTRY and says why; an entry whose mask the index does not hold draws NOT YET MADE. Every panel is drawn over the frame as it opens, whatever input the mask was made on.
   - `R/compare/compose-crops.mjs <picture> <subject> R/final/index.json <out.png>`: the same eight panels as 360 by 360 crops at full size at two places, sixteen crops in four rows of four (rows 1 and 2 place A, rows 3 and 4 place B), 1446 wide and 1494 tall. Place A is the window where the entries' masks cut at a half differ on the most pixels, place B the best window that does not overlap A; each crop has a badge (place letter and panel number) and a white bar exactly 4 pixels wide with a black rim at its bottom left to read a band against. With fewer than two masks standing the header says so and the windows are chosen by edge, or by the samples.
   Four children may run side by side, each writing its own file.
6. `R/compare/index-md.mjs R/final/pictures R/compare/index.md`. Writes the pictures by full path under the judging step that opens each (441, 442, 443), each marked missing if it is not on disk.

## What none of it shows
- The pictures bring the frame down to 685 pixels wide for the comparison picture, so a band of a few pixels is the crops picture's to show, and the crops hold two places of the frame only.
- The entries are fixed by overlap over union on the planted pictures in coarse form; that figure chooses which variant goes forward and says nothing of a mask on a photograph, which is the judging steps' to read from opened pictures.
- The scene-class kind has no bright-only class map (the kept class maps are for the open and natural-hue inputs), so its two entries are those two inputs.
- A mask index row whose file is missing, or whose size is not the picture's, is drawn as such and is not counted.
