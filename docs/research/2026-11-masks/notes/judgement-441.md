## What this judgement cannot show

- Region sizes and band widths are read by eye from the pictures, not counted; a region near a hundredth of the frame or a band near four pixels could be read the other way by a count.
- Each frame-subject has crops at two places only, chosen where the masks differ most; every other edge is seen at the comparison panel's scale, about four frame pixels to one panel pixel, where a band of four pixels cannot be seen. So "not met" rests on faults seen; where a panel's faults all lie at one place, the rest of its edges are not proved to meet the bar.
- A faint weight over the pale conifers of NIR_1827 cannot be told from their own colour as they open.
- The reading that counts a plainly visible tint under a half as taken in part decides one panel on its own: the recipe's on NIR_3461 Foliage. At the half cut alone that panel shows no wrong region at the panel's scale, and its horizon edge lies in no crop, so on a reading that counts only the half cut it is unjudged from these pictures rather than met.
- Three frames of eight; NIR_1651, NIR_3742, NIR_3705, NIR_3710 and NIR_1873 are steps 442 and 443's.
- Nothing here says how a mask would look after an edit through it, how fast a tablet makes it, how much memory it takes, or what a carried mask does on a second photograph.
- Whether another edge stage, another variant or more samples would meet the result is not shown; only the entries drawn were judged.
