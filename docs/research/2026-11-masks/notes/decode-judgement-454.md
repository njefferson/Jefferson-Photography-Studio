# Judgement of step 454: the masks from the decode beside the masks from the drawn picture, NIR_3461, NIR_3466, NIR_1827 and NIR_1651

Each picture was opened, from the index by its path, before any step's note or report was read; no note or report of another step was read at all. Every mark below is read from the opened picture and from nothing else. The coverage figures printed in the panel titles were not used.

## How the bar was read

- Region clause: met where no connected region wrongly taken, and none wrongly left, is larger than one hundredth of the frame. The frame is 2,800 by 1,864, so one hundredth is about 52,000 pixels, a square of about 230 frame pixels. The panels are drawn at about 5.5 frame pixels to a panel pixel, so that square is about 42 panel pixels across. Every size below is an estimate by eye from the panels, written as about so many hundredths of the frame.
- Edge clause: met where, at each of the three places A, B and C, the line of the mask cut at a half lies within 4 pixels of the thing's own edge, read in the crops at the frame's own size against the white mark 4 pixels wide. Where a line lies where the thing has no edge (in open sky, in a road), its distance is given to the nearest edge of the thing. Thin structures about two marks wide or less (pylon braces, cables, painted lines, fence wire) taken in or left out are counted under the region clause.
- Only the mask cut at a half is judged, as the yellow lines on the panels and the lines in the crops show it; the tint below a half is not marked.
- The word for the decode way against the drawn way: better where its mask is seen nearer the subject's region or edge in the panels or crops with nothing seen pulling the other way as much; worse the reverse; same where no difference is seen or the differences pull both ways about equally; broken only for a mask that is empty, the whole frame, or noise with no region.

## What this judgement cannot show

- The device's own drawing. These pictures were composed in the container from masks a node harness made by calling the app's modules; neither the test page nor the device's graphics chip drew anything here, so how the page draws these masks on the device is not seen.
- That the harness's decode way is the page's own call. The masks are taken as given; no step's report was read and the wiring was not checked.
- That a mask from the decode stays the same under a change of white balance, exposure or the red and blue swap. Each picture shows one setting; a side by side at one setting does not test constancy.
- The soft part of either mask. Only the mask cut at a half is judged; how the tint below a half would act on an edit is not marked.
- Exact sizes and distances. Sizes are estimates by eye from panels at about 5.5 frame pixels to a panel pixel, so a region near one hundredth could fall either side; edge distances are read against the 4 px mark, and on feathery needle tips and soft cloud edges 4 px against 8 is at the limit of the eye.
- Faults away from the three places. Each mask's edge is seen at frame size only at three crops; elsewhere it is seen only at panel size.
- The thing's own edge where the bare frame is itself unclear: the gap in NIR_1651's branch and the sparse branches of NIR_1827's snag are read as sky behind thin branches, which is a reading.
- Independence. The sky and foliage masks of NIR_1827 and of NIR_1651 share one boundary, so each frame's two words rest on the same evidence.
- The other four frames and the copied recipes, which step 455 judges.
- Which mask a person editing would prefer; the words say only which lies nearer the subject the samples name, by the bar.
