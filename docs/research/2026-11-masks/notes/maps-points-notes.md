## How the models were run (for the readers of the scores)
- The picture is resized to a longest side of 1024 (1024 by 682) with a triangle filter widened by the shrink, as the picture library's bilinear resize does; the plant for the resize height read 682.
- SlimSAM: normalised by the preprocessor file's mean and spread, padded with zero to 1024 by 1024, encoded once; points given in resized coordinates; the best-scored of the three masks taken; its 256 by 256 scores brought to 1024 by 1024 by bilinear interpolation, cropped to the resized area, brought to 2,800 by 1,864 by bilinear interpolation, then sigmoid and byte. The export script's own handling of the padding point is inside the ONNX file; none was added by this step.
- MobileSAM: the encoder file takes the resized picture as height, width, 3 (values 0 to 255) and normalises and pads inside; the decoder takes points in resized coordinates, a padding point of label minus one, no mask input, and the original size, and gives logits at the picture's size (the decoder's own resize); then sigmoid and byte. The decoder file's logits ranged about minus 14 to plus 18, consistent with logits.
- Two ways of giving the samples: joint (all of a subject's sample centres inside, every other name's centres outside, one prompt) and each (one prompt for each sample, that centre inside and the other names' centres outside, the masks joined by the larger weight at each pixel).
- Three inputs: as it opens, natural hue, bright-only. The bright-only pictures were not on disk beside the others when this step reached them, so this step made all fifteen by the plan's rule under maps-points/inputs-bright (a pixel on the pink side takes the green direction by a smooth weight from 0 at brightness 0.40 to 1 at 0.60; the rest as step 423's natural-hue script). For the planted pictures the axis is the one found on each planted picture itself, not on the frames its windows came from; these are this step's own and may differ from step 438's.
- Only the first sample set of a picture is used where the samples file holds several (NIR_3710's second set of 10 November is not used).
- Masks: 12 SlimSAM and MobileSAM parts, 192 planted masks and 264 frame masks (22 frame subjects, three inputs, two ways, two models) in R/maps-points, each named model-way-input-picture-subject.png, with index.json and run-log-<model>.json beside them.

## Times (this machine, plain processor)
- SlimSAM encoder 2.56 s for one picture; MobileSAM encoder 1.75 s (plant-straight, the first picture of each). A subject's joint decode 0.3 to 0.9 s with the mask brought to size. A part of eight frames on one input ran in 53 to 88 s. These are this container's figures and not a tablet's.
