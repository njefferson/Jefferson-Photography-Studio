# Teaching gallery — shareable low-res IR frames

Eight of Noah Jefferson's infrared frames (Nikon Z50, IR-converted, red filter),
downscaled to a **1600 px long edge, JPEG q80** so users have plenty to play with
without giving away the full-resolution originals. Originals are **not** stored in
this repo — only these low-res copies ship / deploy.

These complement the three RAW lesson files (`../canopy.dng`, `../lodge.dng`,
`../hillside.dng`): the DNGs teach the true-RAW, sub-2000K white-balance path;
this gallery is the free, shareable "here, go play" set covering every color-IR
teaching surface.

**`NIR_1701.jpg`** — landscape.
A stand of "white forest" conifers under a streaky sky.
Teaches white balance, the R and B swap, Looks, and foliage saturation.

**`NIR_1706.jpg`** — PORTRAIT.
Dense conifer forest with one tall dead snag.
Teaches foliage texture and clarity, and a portrait frame through Batch.

**`NIR_1716.jpg`** — landscape.
A big tree at the right, swirling cloud sky, snag at centre.
Teaches the sky mask, per-colour grading, and composition.

**`NIR_1721.jpg`** — landscape.
Lakeside forest with its reflection, two contrails, distant people and boats.
Teaches white balance on water against foliage, the sky mask over contrails, and
heal or clone when those arrive.

**`NIR_1808.jpg`** — PORTRAIT.
A backlit single-pine foliage close-up against a dark sky.
Teaches foliage detail, the Colour mixer on one hue, and clarity and texture.

**`NIR_1864.jpg`** — landscape.
Weeping conifer branches filling the frame.
Teaches foliage texture, the Colour mixer on foliage, and clarity.

**`NIR_1866.jpg`** — landscape.
Looking up through the canopy, with a bold dark trunk at the left.
Teaches trunk and bark texture, the hot-spot, and foliage detail.

**`NIR_1825.jpg`** — landscape.
A popcorn cloudscape over a tree line, snag at centre.
Teaches the sky mask, dehaze, the tone curve, and cloud detail.

**`NIR_1827.jpg`** — landscape.
A dramatic wispy red sky over a tree line with a tall snag.
Teaches the tone curve, dehaze, and a big smooth-sky mask.

**`NIR_1665.jpg`** — landscape.
Two evergreens framing a deep-red sky with a cloud band.
Teaches tap white balance on foliage against sky, the swap, and per-colour.

## Second camera — full-spectrum Nikon D5300 (magenta look)

Four frames from a **full-spectrum-converted Nikon D5300** wearing a
**lens-mounted IR filter** (exact wavelength unknown; white-balanced in-camera),
a distinctly **magenta/purple** palette instead of the red-filter Z50 look above.
Two reasons they earn a place: a *second color world* from a different capture
(so white balance and the R⇄B swap teach differently on a full-spectrum + lens-
filter source vs. a red-filter one) and the first **non-forest subject** in the
set (the urban hillside town). Same 1600 px / q80 low-res treatment.

**`magenta-woodland.jpg`.** Magenta woodland, bare branches, water.
Teaches dense-scene white balance, texture, and reflection.

**`magenta-fir.jpg`.** A dark fir among bare branches, near-black.
Teaches low-key tone, shadows, and a strong black-and-white conversion.

**`magenta-hilltown.jpg`.** An urban hillside: houses, cars, cypress, flat sky.
Teaches white balance on man-made and neutral surfaces, and the sky mask on a
new hue.

**`magenta-dusk-trees.jpg`.** A backlit conifer and weeping tree at dusk, in
crimson.
Teaches foliage detail and moody tone.

Each frame carries a small **bottom-right watermark** — the NJ mark plus
`jefferson-photo-studio.pages.dev` — over a subtle scrim, so a photo shared out
of the tutorial still points back to the tool. It's deliberately corner-only
(not across the picture) and croppable by design.
